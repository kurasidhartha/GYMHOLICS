## ⚡ What It Does

Enter your stats → get a fully personalized plan in seconds.

- 🔥 **Live TDEE Calculator** — BMR, calorie target & macros update as you type
- 🍗 **Custom Meal Plan** — 6 meals/day scaled to your exact calorie needs
- 🏋️ **5-Day Workout Split** — Push / Pull / Legs / Upper / Lower with progressive overload cues
- 📈 **Transformation Projections** — 4, 8, 12 & 24-week milestones
- 🗓️ **Daily Tracker** — Log weight, calories & steps (saves in your browser)
- 💬 **Legends Wall** — Quotes from Arnold, Ronnie, Dorian, Jay, Phil & Kai
